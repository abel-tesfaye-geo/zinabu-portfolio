![Zinabu Getahun Sisay]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/zinabu.jpg.jpeg){: .align-left width="220"}

Head of the Land Administration and Surveying department at Bahir Dar University. His work asks one question: how accurate is the spatial data behind land registration? He combines GNSS, aerial photogrammetry and field surveying to test orthophotos and line maps for cadastral and planning use, and has coordinated the technical verification of orthophotos for Ethiopian cities at national scale. He is a partner researcher in the Edu4GEO and Li4LaM projects with BOKU and TU Wien, developing geomatics curricula and training for land administration.
