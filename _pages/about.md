I am the Head of the Land Administration and Surveying department at Bahir Dar University. My work asks one question: how accurate is the spatial data behind land registration?

I combine GNSS, aerial photogrammetry and field surveying to test orthophotos and line maps for cadastral and planning use, and I have coordinated the technical verification of orthophotos for Ethiopian cities at national scale.

I am a partner researcher in the Edu4GEO and Li4LaM projects with BOKU and TU Wien, developing geomatics curricula and training for land administration.
